Ask Us Anything ↗
How long does a typical website project take?
+
Most websites take 2–4 weeks from kickoff to launch. Complex web apps or e-commerce projects with custom features can take 6–10 weeks. We always give you a clear timeline upfront in the proposal.
Do I need technical knowledge to work with you?
+
Not at all. We handle everything — from strategy and design to development and launch. You just need to show up for the discovery call and provide feedback along the way. We keep things simple and jargon-free.
What happens after my project launches?
+
All packages include a post-launch support period. After that, we offer monthly retainer plans covering updates, performance monitoring, security patches, and ongoing SEO/growth work.
Can I update my website myself after launch?
+
Yes. We build with user-friendly CMS options so you can make content updates easily. We also provide a training session post-launch so you're comfortable managing your site independently.
Do you work with international clients?
+
Yes, absolutely. While we're based in Mumbai, we serve clients across India, UAE, UK, USA, and Australia. All communication and project management happens seamlessly online.
Can I combine multiple services in one project?
+
Yes. Most of our clients combine web design with SEO and AI chatbot integration for maximum impact. We offer discounted bundled pricing when you combine services. Just mention your requirements on the discovery call.
What industries do you specialise in?
+
We work across e-commerce, real estate, healthcare, education, professional services, manufacturing, and fintech. Our Thane and Mumbai clients span from local retail chains to enterprise B2B firms. Our adaptable methodology works for any industry.
Do you provide hosting and domain services?
+
We recommend and manage hosting infrastructure on AWS, Vercel, and premium providers. We can handle domain registration, SSL certificates, CDN setup, and ongoing hosting management as part of our maintenance packages.
Can you work within my existing tech stack?
+
Absolutely. We regularly integrate with existing platforms including Shopify, WordPress, Zoho, HubSpot, Salesforce, and custom-built systems. We adapt to your environment rather than forcing you into ours.
Is there a minimum budget to work with Automora?
+
We take projects starting from ₹25,000. For smaller needs, we can recommend specific services or point you to helpful resources. Every potential client gets a free discovery call where we discuss scope and budget openly.
