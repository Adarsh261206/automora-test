+
We primarily work with modern stacks: React, Next.js, TypeScript, Node.js, Python, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, and cloud platforms like AWS and Vercel. We choose the best technology for each project's specific requirements.

Yes. We offer post-launch support and maintenance for all software projects. Our retainer plans cover bug fixes, updates, performance monitoring, feature additions, and infrastructure management.
