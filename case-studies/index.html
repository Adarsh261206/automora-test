Challenge: Digital Cards Academy needed a modern, high-converting web presence to showcase their digital business card solutions to a growing B2B audience. Solution: We rebuilt the entire platform with a clean, conversion-focused design using React and Next.js. The solution was architected by our CTO Adarsh Sharma for optimal performance.

Results: The new site drove significant improvements in user engagement and lead conversion.
