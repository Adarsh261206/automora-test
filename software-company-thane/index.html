How do you approach custom software development?
+
We follow a structured 5-phase process: Discovery (understanding your business and requirements), Design (prototyping and UX), Development (agile sprints with weekly demos), Testing (comprehensive QA), and Deployment (launch with monitoring). We involve you at every stage to ensure alignment.
How much does custom software cost?
+
Costs vary based on complexity, features, and timeline. A simple web application might start from ₹50,000, while enterprise systems can range higher. We provide a detailed proposal with transparent pricing after understanding your requirements during a free consultation.
Can you integrate with our existing tools and software?
+
Yes. We specialise in building integrations with popular platforms like Shopify, WordPress, Salesforce, Zoho, Google Workspace, and custom APIs. We'll assess your current tech stack and build seamless connections between your systems.
What if I need changes after the software is built?
+
That's expected and welcome. We build software iteratively and can continue adding features post-launch. Many of our clients have ongoing retainer agreements for continuous improvement and new feature development as their business evolves.
Do you provide hosting and infrastructure management?
+
Absolutely. We deploy software on AWS, Vercel, or DigitalOcean and offer ongoing infrastructure management. This includes server monitoring, scaling, backups, security patches, and performance optimisation to keep your application running reliably.
What technologies do you use for software development?
+
We use modern, proven technologies: React, Next.js, TypeScript, Node.js, Python, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Redis, Docker, and cloud platforms like AWS and Vercel. We choose the stack that best fits your project's specific needs — not a one-size-fits-all approach.
How long does a typical software project take?
+
Timelines depend on complexity. A basic MVP can be ready in 4–6 weeks. A full-featured CRM or enterprise system typically takes 3–6 months. We provide a detailed roadmap with milestones before development begins, so you always know what to expect.
Can you build a mobile app alongside the web software?
+
Yes. We develop cross-platform mobile apps using React Native alongside your web application, sharing the same backend and database. This gives your team and customers access to the same data from any device — phone, tablet, or desktop.
