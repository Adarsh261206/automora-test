How quickly do you respond to enquiries?
+
We typically respond within 24 hours, and usually much faster — often within a few hours during business days. For urgent enquiries, we recommend reaching out via WhatsApp or phone for the fastest response.
Can I visit your office for a meeting?
+
We primarily operate as a remote-first studio, but we're happy to arrange in-person meetings at co-working spaces or cafes in Mumbai and Thane. We believe great ideas can spark anywhere — and conversations over chai often lead to the best outcomes.
Do you work with clients outside India?
+
Absolutely. We serve clients across India, UAE, UK, USA, Australia, and beyond. All communication and project management happens seamlessly online — we're comfortable working across time zones and have experience with international collaborations.
What information should I prepare before contacting you?
+
Just your ideas and goals. If you have a project brief, budget range, or examples of websites you admire — bring them along. But it's absolutely fine if you're still in the early stages. We'll guide the conversation and help you clarify your vision.
Is there a cost for the initial consultation?
+
No. The initial discovery call is completely free with no obligation. We believe in demonstrating value before asking for commitment. If we're not the right fit, we'll be honest about it and point you in the right direction.
What happens after I submit the contact form?
+
We receive your message and review it personally — usually Adarsh (our CTO) responds directly. We'll acknowledge receipt within a few hours and schedule a discovery call at your earliest convenience. From there, we follow our three-step process to get you from idea to launch.
