WhatsApp Business API vs.
Regular WhatsApp App

Understand the difference between the free WhatsApp Business App and the professional Business API.

WhatsApp Business App (Free)

Manual & Limited

Single device only. No automation, no chatbots, no CRM sync. Limited to 256 broadcast recipients. Manual replies required. No analytics or reporting. Best for very small home businesses with low message volume.

✦ Recommended

WhatsApp Business API

Multi-agent support with unlimited devices. Full automation with AI chatbots, broadcast campaigns (unlimited recipients), CRM integration, analytics dashboard, green tick verification, and 24/7 customer support automation. Used by 80% of top Indian brands for customer communication.

What is the difference between WhatsApp Business API and the regular app?
+
The WhatsApp Business API is designed for medium to large businesses needing automation at scale. It supports chatbots, broadcasts, CRM integration, multiple agents, and analytics — unlike the free Business App which is manual and limited to a single device.
How long does it take to set up WhatsApp automation?
+
API onboarding and verification typically takes 1–2 weeks. Chatbot development and flow design adds 1–3 weeks depending on complexity. We provide a detailed timeline during the proposal stage.
Can WhatsApp automation integrate with my existing CRM?
+
Yes. We integrate WhatsApp automation with popular CRMs like Zoho, Salesforce, HubSpot, and custom systems. Conversations are logged, contacts updated, and workflows triggered automatically based on WhatsApp interactions.
Is WhatsApp automation compliant with data privacy regulations?
+
Absolutely. Our solutions comply with WhatsApp's Business Policy and data protection regulations. We implement opt-in flows, consent management, and secure data handling practices to ensure full compliance.
What kind of businesses benefit from WhatsApp automation?
+
E-commerce stores, real estate agencies, healthcare providers, educational institutions, travel companies, logistics firms, and professional services — any business that communicates with customers at scale can benefit from WhatsApp automation.
How much does WhatsApp Business API cost?
+
WhatsApp charges per conversation (typically ₹0.30–₹0.90 per conversation depending on region and template type). Beyond that, our setup and management fees range from ₹15,000 for basic setup to ₹1.5L+ for enterprise-grade automation with full CRM integration. We provide transparent pricing in your proposal.
Can I send marketing messages via WhatsApp API?
+
Yes, within WhatsApp's policy framework. You need prior opt-in from recipients, and marketing messages must use approved message templates. We handle template creation, approval, and compliance for your campaigns. Promotional broadcasts achieve 90%+ open rates with proper targeting.
What happens if my chatbot can't answer a customer question?
+
Our chatbots are designed with intelligent escalation flows. When a query exceeds the bot's capability, it seamlessly transfers the conversation to a human agent with full context. No customer is left hanging, and your team only handles complex issues that truly need human intervention.
