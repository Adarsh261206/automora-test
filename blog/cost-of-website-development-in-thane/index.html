Key Factors That Influence Website Development Cost in Thane

Not all websites cost the same — here are the major factors that drive pricing up or down:

Number of Pages & Content Complexity

A 5-page site is significantly cheaper than a 20-page site because each page requires design mockups, content integration, and responsive testing. If you need custom illustrations, animations, or interactive elements, expect the price to increase by 20–40%.

Design Requirements

A custom UI/UX design costs more than using a pre-built template. In Thane, custom design typically adds Rs. 10,000–25,000 to the project. However, it gives you a unique brand identity that sets you apart from competitors who use the same ThemeForest template.

Functionality & Features

Need a payment gateway? A booking system? Multi-language support? Each feature adds development time. A simple contact form takes a few hours to integrate, but a custom booking engine with calendar sync and SMS notifications can take weeks.

SEO & Performance Optimisation

Basic SEO — meta tags, sitemaps, semantic HTML — should be included in any reputable development package. Advanced SEO work like schema markup, Core Web Vitals optimisation, and content strategy is typically billed separately or as part of a monthly retainer.

Custom vs Template: What You Actually Pay For

Many businesses in Thane ask whether they should go with a custom website or a template-based site. Here's the honest truth: if your budget is under Rs. 15,000, a well-customised template is often the best option. If you have Rs. 35,000 or more, custom development delivers significantly better long-term value.

Custom websites are built from the ground up — every line of code, every design decision is made for your specific business needs. This means faster load times, better security (no vulnerable third-party plugins), easier scalability, and a design that genuinely reflects your brand. Adarsh Sharma, our co-founder and technical architect, emphasises that custom builds also perform better in SEO because the code is clean, semantic, and optimised from day one.

Templates, on the other hand, are cheaper upfront but can cost more in the long run. You'll pay for the template license, premium plugins, customisation work to make it look unique, and ongoing maintenance as the template framework updates. Over 3 years, a template site often ends up costing nearly as much as a custom build.

Hidden Costs to Watch Out For

When budgeting for your website, don't forget these often-overlooked expenses:

  • Domain Name: Rs. 700–1,500/year for a .com or .in domain
  • Hosting: Rs. 3,000–12,000/year for reliable hosting (cheap shared hosting hurts performance)
  • SSL Certificate: Often free with hosting, but premium EV certificates can cost Rs. 10,000+/year
  • Content Creation: Professional copywriting and photography can add Rs. 10,000–30,000
  • Ongoing Maintenance: Budget Rs. 2,500–10,000/month for updates, backups, and security
  • Digital Marketing: A website without traffic is like a shop with no sign. Budget for SEO or Google Ads separately
