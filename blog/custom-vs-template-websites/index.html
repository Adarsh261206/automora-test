Introduction

One of the most common questions we hear from business owners in Thane and Mumbai is: "Should I buy a template or build a custom website?" It's a critical decision that affects your budget, timeline, branding, SEO performance, and long-term flexibility.

The short answer: it depends on your goals, budget, and growth plans. In this guide, we break down everything you need to know about custom vs template websites — with real data, cost comparisons, and honest advice from someone who has built both types for over 50 businesses. At Automora Tech Solutions, we've helped businesses across Thane make this decision with confidence.

What Are Template Websites?

A template website uses a pre-designed layout that you customise with your own content, colours, and images. Templates are purchased from marketplaces like ThemeForest, TemplateMonster, or provided by platforms like WordPress, Wix, or Squarespace.

Pros of template websites:

  • Lower upfront cost: Templates cost Rs. 2,000–8,000, and setup can be done for Rs. 10,000–20,000
  • Faster launch: A template site can go live in 1–2 weeks
  • Predictable design: You see exactly what you're getting before you buy
  • Ecosystem support: Plugins and community support for common platforms

Cons of template websites:

  • Generic appearance: Your site may look like many others — thousands of businesses buy the same template
  • Bloated code: Templates include code for features you don't need, slowing down your site
  • Limited customisation: You're restricted by the template's framework and plugin limitations
  • SEO limitations: Templates often have poor semantic structure and unnecessary CSS/JS that hurt Core Web Vitals
  • Plugin dependency: Each plugin adds security risk and potential compatibility issues

What Are Custom Websites?

A custom website is built from scratch — every line of code, every design element is created specifically for your business. The design is unique, the code is clean, and the architecture is built around your specific needs.

Pros of custom websites:

  • Unique design: No other business will have a site that looks like yours
  • Clean, semantic code: Better SEO performance from day one
  • Scalable architecture: Built to grow with your business
  • No bloat: Only the features you need — no unnecessary code
  • Better security: No vulnerable third-party plugins to exploit
  • Full ownership: You own the code and can take it anywhere

Cons of custom websites:

  • Higher upfront cost: Rs. 25,000+ for a basic custom site
  • Longer timeline: 3–8 weeks depending on complexity
  • Requires professional team: You need experienced developers and designers

Cost Analysis: Upfront vs Long-Term

Here's where most businesses get it wrong. They look at the upfront cost without considering the total cost of ownership (TCO) over 3 years. Let's do the math:

Template site (Rs. 15,000 upfront): Template license (Rs. 4,000) + customisation (Rs. 11,000) + premium plugins (Rs. 8,000/year × 3 = Rs. 24,000) + template updates (Rs. 3,000/year × 3 = Rs. 9,000) + annual hosting (Rs. 6,000/year × 3 = Rs. 18,000) = Total: Rs. 70,000 over 3 years

Custom site (Rs. 45,000 upfront): Development (Rs. 45,000) + annual hosting (Rs. 6,000/year × 3 = Rs. 18,000) + maintenance (Rs. 2,500/month × 36 = Rs. 90,000, or optional self-managed) = Total: Rs. 63,000 over 3 years (with maintenance) or Rs. 63,000 without

As you can see, the gap narrows significantly over 3 years. And when you factor in the SEO advantage (a custom site ranking on page 1 vs a template on page 3), the ROI gap widens in favour of custom development.

When to Choose Each Option

Choose a template website if:

  • Your budget is under Rs. 20,000
  • You need a site live in under 2 weeks
  • You're testing a business idea and don't want to invest heavily yet
  • You don't need advanced features or integrations
  • SEO isn't a primary traffic source for your business

Choose a custom website if:

  • You're investing in long-term brand building
  • SEO and organic traffic are important to your business
  • You need unique functionality or integrations
  • You plan to scale and add features over time
  • Your business competes in a crowded market (like Mumbai or Thane)
  • You want full ownership and control of your code

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a custom and template website?
+
A custom website is built from scratch with unique design and code tailored to your business. A template website uses a pre-designed layout that is customised with your content. Custom sites offer more flexibility, better SEO, and unique branding, while templates are cheaper and faster to deploy.
Are template websites bad for SEO?
+
Not inherently, but they often come with bloated code, unnecessary CSS/JS, and poor semantic structure — all of which hurt SEO. Custom websites are built with clean, semantic HTML and can be optimised for Core Web Vitals from day one, giving them a significant SEO advantage.
Can I upgrade from a template to a custom website later?
+
Yes, but it's essentially a full rebuild. You cannot simply upgrade a template — you'll need to design and develop a new site from scratch. This is why many businesses find it more cost-effective to start with a custom build if they have long-term growth plans.
How much more does a custom website cost than a template?
+
A custom website typically costs 40–80% more upfront. However, when you factor in template license fees, premium plugins, customisation work to make it unique, and ongoing maintenance, the 3-year total cost of ownership is often comparable between the two options.
