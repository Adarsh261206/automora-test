Introduction

Every business owner in Thane considering digital marketing faces the same question: SEO vs Google Ads — which should I invest in? The answer isn't straightforward because both strategies have different strengths, timelines, and cost structures.

In this guide, we compare SEO vs Google Ads across every important dimension — cost, timeline, ROI, risk, and scalability — based on real data from campaigns we've run at Automora Tech Solutions for businesses in Thane and Mumbai.

Cost Comparison

SEO costs for a Thane business: A comprehensive SEO campaign typically costs Rs. 15,000–40,000 per month. This includes technical SEO, content creation, link building, local SEO, and reporting. The value builds over time — a page ranking #1 for a high-value keyword might generate Rs. 50,000–1,00,000 in monthly revenue without additional ad spend.

Google Ads costs for a Thane business: Average cost-per-click in India ranges from Rs. 15–200 depending on industry. Legal and insurance keywords can exceed Rs. 500 per click. A Thane-based service business typically spends Rs. 15,000–50,000 per month on Google Ads. Once you stop spending, traffic stops immediately.

Over a 3-year period, SEO's total cost is typically 40–50% lower than Google Ads for the same volume of traffic — but it takes longer to build.

Timeline & Results

SEO: It takes 3–6 months to start seeing meaningful results. But here's the key insight — SEO traffic compounds. Month 6 might bring 1,000 visitors, month 12 could bring 5,000, and month 24 could bring 20,000. Each piece of content you create continues generating traffic for years.

Google Ads: Results are immediate. You can launch a campaign today and get clicks within hours. This makes Google Ads excellent for seasonal campaigns, new product launches, and testing new markets. However, the traffic is transactional — it exists only as long as you pay for it.

ROI Analysis for Thane Businesses

Let's look at real numbers from a Thane-based home services company we worked with:

SEO investment (12 months): Rs. 2,40,000 total (Rs. 20,000/month). Traffic: 15,000 organic visits/month by month 12. Conversions: 200+ leads/month. Cost per lead: Rs. 100 (dropping each month as traffic compounds).

Google Ads investment (12 months): Rs. 3,60,000 total (Rs. 30,000/month). Traffic: 8,000 ad clicks/month. Conversions: 150+ leads/month. Cost per lead: Rs. 200 (relatively stable month to month).

The combined approach delivered the best results: Rs. 50,000/month total spend generated 350+ leads — a blended cost per lead of Rs. 143. For most Thane businesses, the answer to the SEO vs Google Ads question isn't "which one" — it's "both."

"We started with Google Ads to get immediate leads, then built our SEO alongside. After 9 months, SEO was generating more leads than ads at a fraction of the cost. Now we use ads only for new service launches and retargeting." — Automora client, Thane-based home services company

When to Use Each Strategy

Choose SEO if:

  • You're playing the long game (3+ year horizon)
  • You want sustainable, compound growth
  • Your customers research before buying
  • You have a limited monthly budget
  • You're building a brand, not just selling

Choose Google Ads if:

  • You need customers right now
  • You're testing a new market or product
  • You have seasonal offers or promotions
  • Your industry has high search intent (e.g., "emergency plumber")
  • You want to retarget website visitors

The Hybrid Approach

The most successful digital marketers use both. Here's a framework we recommend for Thane businesses:

  1. Month 1–3: Start Google Ads for immediate traffic. Use this time to identify which keywords convert best
  2. Month 1–6: Build SEO foundation — technical audit, content strategy, local SEO, link building
  3. Month 6+: Reduce ad spend as SEO traffic grows. Use Google Ads for retargeting and new keyword testing
  4. Ongoing: Reinvest savings from reduced ad spend into content and SEO expansion

This hybrid approach minimises upfront risk while maximising long-term ROI. Adarsh Sharma recommends this strategy for most service-based businesses in Thane.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is better — SEO or Google Ads?
+
Neither is universally better. SEO is best for long-term, sustainable traffic. Google Ads is best for immediate results and testing. Most successful businesses use both strategies together.
How much does Google Ads cost in India?
+
Google Ads cost-per-click in India ranges from Rs. 15–200. Local service businesses in Thane typically spend Rs. 15,000–50,000 per month. Highly competitive industries like insurance or real estate can exceed these ranges.
How long does SEO take to work?
+
SEO typically takes 3–6 months to show meaningful results. The traffic compounds over time — unlike ads where traffic stops when you stop paying. SEO's long-term ROI is 2.8x higher than paid advertising.
Can I do SEO and Google Ads together?
+
Absolutely. Use Google Ads for immediate traffic and testing keywords, then invest SEO budget in keywords that convert well. The combined effect is greater than either channel alone. This is the strategy we recommend for most businesses.
