Introduction

Building a website can feel overwhelming — especially if you're not a developer. Between choosing a platform, designing layouts, writing code, and finally launching, there are dozens of steps that can confuse even seasoned business owners. That's why understanding the website development process is crucial before you start.

At Automora Tech Solutions, we've refined our development workflow over 50+ client projects. This guide walks you through every stage — from the first discovery call to the final launch and beyond. Whether you're working with a Thane-based web developer or building in-house, knowing the process helps you plan better, communicate clearly, and get a website that actually works for your business.

Phase 1: Discovery & Planning

This is the most important phase — and the one most often skipped by inexperienced teams. During discovery, we answer fundamental questions:

  • What is the primary goal of the website? (Generate leads? Sell products? Share information?)
  • Who is the target audience? Demographics, behaviours, pain points
  • What are the key features needed? Contact forms, payment gateways, booking systems, member areas
  • Who are the competitors? We analyse 3–5 competitor websites to identify opportunities
  • What is the budget and timeline? Realistic expectations set upfront

The output of this phase is a project brief — a document that serves as the single source of truth for the entire project. It includes a sitemap, wireframes, technical specifications, and a content plan. A well-prepared brief can save weeks of back-and-forth later in the process.

Phase 2: UI/UX Design

Once the planning is locked, the design team creates visual mockups. This phase typically includes:

Wireframing

Low-fidelity layouts that focus on structure, hierarchy, and user flow — without visual polish. Wireframes are fast to create and iterate on, making them essential for catching usability issues early.

Visual Design

High-fidelity mockups in tools like Figma. This is where typography, colour schemes, imagery, and spacing come together. At Automora, we design mobile-first — meaning the mobile layout is created before the desktop version. 65% of web traffic in India comes from mobile devices, so designing for small screens first is non-negotiable.

Pro tip: Involve your stakeholders in design reviews early. A sign-off at the design stage prevents costly rework during development. Each round of design revisions saves roughly 8–10 hours of development time.

Phase 3: Development & Coding

With approved designs in hand, the development team starts building. This phase splits into two tracks:

Frontend Development

The frontend is what users see and interact with. We build using modern frameworks and clean HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Every component is tested across Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. We also ensure WCAG 2.1 accessibility standards — alt text on images, proper heading hierarchy, keyboard navigation, and colour contrast compliance.

Backend Development

The backend handles data processing, user authentication, content management, and integrations. For most business websites, we use lightweight, secure frameworks. For e-commerce, we implement robust inventory management, payment gateway integration (Razorpay, Stripe, or PhonePe), and order tracking systems.

Throughout development, we use version control (Git) to track every change. This means we can roll back to any previous version if something breaks — a critical safety net that many small agencies don't offer.

Phase 4: Content Integration

A beautiful design means nothing without quality content. During this phase, we populate the site with:

  • Copywritten pages (homepage, about, services, contact)
  • Product descriptions and images (for e-commerce)
  • Blog posts and resource pages
  • Meta titles and descriptions for each page
  • Alt text for all images (important for SEO and accessibility)

If you don't have content ready, most agencies — including ours — can connect you with professional copywriters. Well-written content can improve conversion rates by up to 30% on average.

Phase 5: Testing & QA

Before launch, every feature is tested thoroughly. Our QA checklist includes:

  • Cross-browser testing: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, Opera
  • Mobile responsiveness: Tested on real devices (iPhone, Android, tablets)
  • Performance testing: Google PageSpeed Insights target — 90+ on mobile and desktop
  • Form testing: Every form submitted and verified (including email delivery)
  • Link checking: No broken internal or external links
  • Security scan: Vulnerability checks, SSL verification, SQL injection testing
  • SEO audit: Proper meta tags, schema markup, sitemap, robots.txt

If you're working with a Thane web development company, ask about their QA process. A thorough testing phase can catch 90% of potential issues before they affect your users.

Phase 6: Deployment & Launch

Launch day! The website is migrated from the staging server to the production server. This involves:

  • Configuring the production environment (server, database, CDN)
  • Setting up SSL certificate (HTTPS is mandatory — Google penalises non-HTTPS sites)
  • Pointing the domain name to the new server
  • Submitting the sitemap to Google Search Console
  • Setting up Google Analytics and conversion tracking
  • Final smoke test — every page checked one last time

We typically schedule launches during low-traffic periods (late evening or weekends) so any unexpected issues have minimal impact. A proper launch takes 2–4 hours of focused work.

Phase 7: Post-Launch & Maintenance

A website is never truly "finished". After launch, ongoing maintenance keeps it secure, fast, and relevant. Key activities include:

  • Weekly backups: Automated backups stored off-site
  • Security updates: Patches for CMS, plugins, and server software
  • Performance monitoring: Uptime checks and speed audits
  • Content updates: Adding pages, blog posts, testimonials
  • SEO monitoring: Tracking rankings and adjusting strategy

At Automora Tech Solutions, we offer maintenance packages starting at Rs. 2,500/month for Thane businesses. Regular maintenance prevents the costly emergency fixes that happen when a site gets hacked or crashes.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the website development process take?
+
A typical website takes 4–8 weeks from discovery to launch. Simple 5-page sites can take 2–3 weeks, while complex e-commerce or custom applications take 8–16 weeks. Timelines depend heavily on content readiness and how quickly feedback cycles move.
What is the first step in the web development process?
+
The first step is discovery and planning. Goals are defined, target audiences are analysed, competitor research is conducted, sitemaps are created, and technical requirements are documented. This phase lays the foundation for the entire project.
Do I need content ready before development starts?
+
It's ideal to have core content (homepage, about, services) ready by the design phase. However, development teams can work with placeholder content and add final copy during the content integration phase. Delaying content is one of the most common reasons for project delays.
What happens after a website launches?
+
Post-launch activities include performance monitoring, bug fixes, SEO tracking, analytics review, and ongoing maintenance. Regular updates, security patches, backups, and content refreshes keep your website healthy and ranking well in search results.
How much does the website development process cost?
+
Costs vary based on complexity. A basic 5-page business site ranges from Rs. 15,000–35,000, corporate sites Rs. 35,000–65,000, e-commerce Rs. 60,000–1,20,000, and custom web applications Rs. 1,00,000+. See our detailed pricing guide for a full breakdown.
