Why Ecommerce is Booming in Thane

Thane has emerged as one of the fastest-growing ecommerce markets in the Mumbai Metropolitan Region. With a population of over 1.8 million and rising disposable incomes, Thane businesses are increasingly moving online. According to industry reports, the Indian ecommerce market is projected to reach $350 billion by 2030, and Tier-2 cities like Thane are driving a significant portion of this growth.

At Automora Tech Solutions, we've helped Thane-based retailers, manufacturers, and D2C brands launch their online stores. Whether you're a local boutique in Hiranandani Estate or a speciality food brand in Ghodbunder Road, this guide covers everything you need to build a successful ecommerce store.

Ecommerce Development Costs in Thane

Here's a realistic breakdown of what you can expect to invest:

  • Basic Shopify/WooCommerce store (up to 50 products): Rs. 40,000–70,000 inclusive of theme customisation, payment gateway setup, and product uploads
  • Medium store (50–200 products): Rs. 70,000–1,20,000 with advanced features like inventory management, multi-currency, and reviews
  • Large/custom store (200+ products or custom features): Rs. 1,20,000–3,00,000+
  • Monthly maintenance: Rs. 3,000–8,000 for updates, backups, and support
  • Marketing setup (Google Shopping, Facebook catalog): Rs. 10,000–25,000

Must-Have Features for Your Online Store

Based on our experience building ecommerce stores for Thane clients, here are the features that make the biggest difference to sales:

Mobile-First Design

Over 75% of ecommerce traffic in India comes from mobile devices. Your store must load fast and look great on smartphones. We target a Google PageSpeed score of 85+ on mobile — every second of delay reduces conversion rates by 7%.

Indian Payment Gateways

Integration with Razorpay, PhonePe, Paytm, and UPI is non-negotiable for the Indian market. COD (Cash on Delivery) is still preferred by 40% of Indian online shoppers, so your store must support it.

Product Search & Filters

Good search and filtering can increase conversion rates by up to 30%. Customers should be able to find products by category, price range, brand, size, colour, and ratings.

Customer Reviews & Ratings

Products with reviews have a 270% higher purchase likelihood. Displaying authentic customer reviews builds trust and reduces return rates.

Abandoned Cart Recovery

Cart abandonment rates average 70–80%. Automated email or WhatsApp follow-ups can recover 10–15% of these lost sales. At Automora, we integrate WhatsApp-based cart recovery for our ecommerce clients — a strategy that has recovered up to 22% of abandoned carts for Thane-based stores.

Payment Gateway & Logistics Integration

For Thane-based ecommerce stores, we recommend the following setup:

Payment Gateways: Razorpay (best overall for India), PhonePe for Business, and PayU. All three support UPI, credit/debit cards, net banking, and EMI options. Gateway fees range from 1.5–2.5% per transaction.

Logistics: For Thane businesses, Shiprocket and Delhivery offer excellent last-mile delivery within the MMR region. If you're delivering within Thane city, you can also offer same-day delivery as a competitive advantage — something we've helped several local stores implement.

GST & Invoicing: Your store should auto-generate GST-compliant invoices. Integration with accounting software like Zoho Books or Tally is recommended for businesses handling more than 50 orders per month.

Marketing Your Ecommerce Store

Building the store is only half the battle. Here's how Thane businesses can drive traffic and sales:

  • Google Shopping Ads: Show your products directly in Google search results. Average ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) for Thane-based stores is 4:1 when properly optimised.
  • Instagram & Facebook Shops: With over 350 million Indian users on Instagram, social commerce is huge. Connect your product catalogue directly to your social profiles.
  • Local SEO: Optimise for "buy [product] in Thane" keywords. We've seen 40% of ecommerce traffic come from long-tail local search queries.
  • WhatsApp Marketing: Share product catalogues, offers, and order updates via WhatsApp broadcast lists. See our WhatsApp automation guide for details.
  • Email Marketing: Newsletters, welcome sequences, and abandoned cart emails. Average open rates for ecommerce emails in India are 18–22%.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does an ecommerce website cost in Thane?
+
An ecommerce website in Thane typically costs between Rs. 60,000 and Rs. 1,20,000 for a store with up to 100 products. This includes design, development, payment gateway integration, product uploads, and basic SEO setup.
Which ecommerce platform is best for small businesses?
+
For small businesses in Thane, Shopify is best for ease of use, WooCommerce is best for customisation, and custom-built stores are best for unique requirements. We usually recommend WooCommerce for Thane businesses that want full control without high monthly fees.
How long does it take to build an ecommerce store?
+
A typical ecommerce store takes 5–8 weeks to build. This includes design, product catalogue setup, payment gateway integration, testing, and launch. Complex stores with hundreds of products or custom features can take 8–12 weeks.
Do I need a GST number to sell online?
+
Yes, if your annual turnover exceeds Rs. 20 lakhs (Rs. 10 lakhs for special category states), GST registration is mandatory. Even below the threshold, having a GST number is recommended for B2B sales and input tax credit benefits.
Do you integrate Cash on Delivery?
+
Yes, we integrate COD along with all major Indian payment gateways — Razorpay, PhonePe, PayU, and UPI. COD remains popular in Thane, with approximately 40% of ecommerce orders still placed with cash payment.
