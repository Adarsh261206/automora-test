How do you choose which technologies to use?
+
We select technologies based on project requirements, scalability needs, team expertise, and long-term maintainability. We don't chase trends — we choose tools that are proven, well-supported, and the right fit for your specific use case. Our founder Adarsh Sharma makes the final technology decisions on every project.
Do you work with technologies not on this list?
+
Yes. This list represents our core expertise, but we're constantly exploring and adopting new technologies. If your project requires a specific tool or framework, we'll evaluate it and build a team around it. Our development team is skilled at learning and adapting to new technologies quickly.
Is your technology stack suitable for enterprise projects?
+
Absolutely. Technologies like AWS, PostgreSQL, TypeScript, and Next.js are used by leading enterprises globally. Our stack is designed to scale from small business websites to enterprise-grade applications with thousands of users. We follow security best practices, implement proper testing, and maintain clean, documented codebases.
Do you provide maintenance for existing projects using other tech stacks?
+
Yes. We regularly take over and modernize existing projects built with various technologies. Whether it's a legacy PHP site, an older WordPress build, or a custom application, we can maintain, update, or rebuild it using modern best practices and our proven technology stack.
