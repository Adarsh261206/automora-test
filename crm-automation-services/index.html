+

How long does CRM automation implementation take?
+
Basic CRM automation setups take 1–2 weeks. Full-scale implementations with multiple integrations, custom workflows, and reporting dashboards typically take 3–6 weeks depending on complexity.
Can you automate my existing CRM without migrating data?
+
Yes, in most cases. We build automation on top of your existing CRM without requiring data migration. We connect directly via APIs and build workflows that work with your current setup and data structure.
How do you measure the success of CRM automation?
+
We track key metrics like lead response time, conversion rates, pipeline velocity, tasks automated per week, and time saved by your sales team. Our clients typically see 40–60% reduction in manual CRM work.
Do you provide training for our team on the automated CRM?

What's the difference between CRM automation and a CRM itself?
+
A CRM is a tool for storing customer data and tracking interactions. CRM automation is the layer on top that makes those actions happen automatically — capturing leads from your website, sending follow-up emails, updating deal stages, and generating reports without anyone clicking a button. Think of CRM as the engine and automation as the autopilot.
