About Automora

A Mumbai-based digital studio that builds AI automation, stunning web experiences, and growth systems for brands that refuse to settle for average.

Our Mission ↗ Meet the Founders
Automora Tech Solutions LLP was founded in 2025 by Adarsh Sharma and Saurav Singh — two technologists who believed that digital agencies could deliver world-class craft and measurable business results at the same time. Based in Mumbai, we serve clients across India, UAE, UK, USA, and Australia. We build websites, AI chatbots, WhatsApp automation, CRM pipelines, and SEO strategies that don't just look good — they compound.
Why We Exist

Mission & Vision.

Our Mission
Democratise great digital experiences for every ambitious brand.

We believe that exceptional web design, AI-driven automation, and intelligent growth systems shouldn't be reserved for enterprises with massive budgets. Our mission is to make cutting-edge digital technology accessible to startups, SMEs, and growing brands across India and beyond — without compromising on quality, performance, or results.

Our Vision
To be India's most trusted partner for AI-powered digital growth.

We envision a future where every business — from a local clinic in Thane to a growing enterprise in Mumbai — can harness the power of AI, automation, and purpose-built design to dominate their market. Automora exists to be that bridge between ambition and execution.

Ask Us Anything ↗
When was Automora Tech Solutions founded?
+
Automora Tech Solutions LLP was founded in 2025 by Adarsh Sharma and Saurav Singh. Since our founding, we've shipped over 12 digital projects for clients across India, UAE, UK, USA, and Australia.
Where is Automora located?
+
We are headquartered in Mumbai, Maharashtra, India. But we work with clients globally — from Thane to Toronto — entirely remotely. Our team collaborates across time zones to deliver on schedule.
What makes Automora different from other agencies?
+
We combine three things most agencies don't: world-class web design, real AI automation, and data-driven SEO — all under one roof. Plus, we're obsessive about transparency, craft, and long-term client relationships rather than one-off projects.
Do you work with startups and small businesses?
+
Absolutely. We specifically design our packages to be accessible for startups and growing SMEs. From a basic 5-page website to a full AI-powered growth stack, we have options for every stage of business.
What industries do you specialise in?
+
We work across industries including healthcare, e-commerce, education, insurance, wellness, real estate, and technology. Our process adapts to your specific industry requirements and audience.
Do you offer post-launch support?
+
Yes. Every project includes a post-launch support period. We also offer monthly retainer packages covering updates, performance monitoring, security, SEO maintenance, and ongoing growth work.
