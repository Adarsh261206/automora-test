Brand Story

What Automora
Means

"Automora is more than a name — it's a philosophy. Auto + Mora: automatic momentum. We build systems that keep growing, keep running, keep delivering — without you having to think about them."

Automora was born from a simple observation: most businesses have incredible potential but are held back by manual processes, outdated technology, and fragmented digital experiences. We saw an opportunity to change that — to build a studio that combines deep technical expertise with genuine business understanding.

Founded by Adarsh Sharma and Saurav Singh, Automora started as a two-person team building websites for local businesses in Thane, Mumbai. What began with a single project quickly grew into a full-service digital studio working with clients across India and internationally. Today, Automora Tech Solutions LLP is a recognized name in AI automation, custom web development, and digital growth.

Our name reflects our core promise: automatic momentum for your business. Every solution we build — whether it's an AI chatbot, a CRM integration, a WhatsApp automation system, or a high-performance website — is designed to create self-sustaining growth. We don't just build tools; we build systems that work for you, around the clock, without constant oversight.

What does "Automora" mean?
+
Automora is a fusion of "Auto" (automatic) and "Mora" (momentum). It represents our core philosophy: building systems that create automatic momentum for your business — solutions that keep growing, running, and delivering without constant manual intervention.
Who founded Automora Tech Solutions?
+
Automora was founded by Adarsh Sharma (Co-Founder & CTO) and Saurav Singh (Co-Founder). Adarsh leads the technical vision and engineering, while Saurav drives business development and client relationships.
Where is Automora based?
+
Automora Tech Solutions LLP is headquartered in Thane, Mumbai, Maharashtra, India. We serve clients locally in Mumbai and Thane, as well as remotely across India and internationally.

We offer AI automation, custom web development, WhatsApp chatbot development, CRM integration, technical SEO, and digital growth strategy. Our expertise spans industries including healthcare, education, insurance, e-commerce, and professional services.
